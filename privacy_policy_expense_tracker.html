Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Developer: Hichem Benabadji

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Expense Tracker application ("Expense Tracker") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal finance data.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Expense Tracker does not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal or financial information. Specifically, the App does not:

  • Collect personal or financial data (names, transactions, account balances, emails, etc.)
  • Require an internet connection to manage your expenses
  • Display advertisements
  • Track your location
  • Access sensitive device permissions
  • Share any data with third parties

3. How the App Works

Expense Tracker is a fully local application. All your data (expenses, income, categories, budget history) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted anywhere.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
